users (invite tree)

users are nested by who invited them. banning a user cascade-bans everyone they invited, transitively. to restore a cascade-banned user, unban the ancestor who caused it. you cannot ban another admin (remove them from roles.yaml first) or yourself.

    no users.

    about bans

    a ban is stored only on the directly banned user; cascades are worked out up the invite chain, so unbanning the ancestor restores everyone underneath.

    every ban and unban is appended to bans.log.jsonl beside the user database.

    in-game sessions of a banned user are not kicked: they keep their connection until they reconnect.
